Where light meets life

The best photos aren't just captured they are felt. I chase that fleeting alignment of light, mood, and emotion.

I started TakASnap because I see stories everywhere in the quiet of empty streets at dawn or the curiosity in my cat's gaze. My camera is how I hold onto these moments. These images are personal fragments of my life. I hope they make you feel something, too.
