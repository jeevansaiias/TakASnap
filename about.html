Where light meets life

The best photos aren't taken, they're felt. That split second when light, mood, and emotion align perfectly. That's what I chase.

I started TakASnap because I couldn't stop seeing stories everywhere, in empty streets at dawn, in my cat's curious eyes, in strangers passing by. My camera became how I hold onto those fleeting moments.

These images are personal. Pieces of my life, souls I've connected with. I hope something here makes you feel something too.
